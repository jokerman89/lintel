The full cycle is SENSE, SCOPE, DEFINE, DISCOVER, PLAN, BUILD, REVIEW, SHIP and CAPTURE. Think in three chunks: understand and agree; implement and verify; leave a useful trail.

PLAN aligns the original specification, task map and handoff. You authorize the plan before substantial implementation. BUILD executes coherent packages with short verifiable leaves. REVIEW checks requirements, quality and active-pack compliance. SHIP follows the authorized delivery path. CAPTURE updates useful lessons, decisions and working state; not every cycle needs a new ADR.

These instructions are backed by specific helpers, not a universal scheduler. Content-bound checks validate selected evidence; actual tools and release remain under platform control. Swarming changes how approved packages execute, not the phases or requirements.
