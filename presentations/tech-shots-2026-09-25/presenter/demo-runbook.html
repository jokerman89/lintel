Present the kitchen test

FIVE-MINUTE EVIDENCE SEGMENT / PROTOCOL V2

The Service House kitchen test compares three treatments. It currently has no completed controlled three-arm result in the inspected evidence.

Before the session

  1. Open the public kitchen guide. It works without the separate harness.
  2. Confirm the expected result from K14, K20 and K25. Know which screen is a requirement, which is calibration and which is an actual candidate result.
  3. If demonstrating a local reference fixture, verify it in advance and label it “reference calibration.” Do not substitute it for Naked, Lintel or Swarming.
  4. Use a reviewed, sanitized result export only after a real cohort exists. A stale “running” record is not current runtime evidence.

The five-minute stage path

TimeActionSay
0:00–1:30Show the three treatments and fixed brief.“Same product contract. The workflow treatment changes.”
1:30–3:30Trace exact cents, cancel-once and reload-once. Optional: a clearly labelled calibration fixture.“These are the behaviors we inspect in every candidate.”
3:30–5:00Show the separate measures and current evidence status.“The controlled comparison is not complete. There is no winner to announce.”

What changed in v2

The controller serves the app. The candidate works in public/, follows the shared runtime, then submits once with BENCHMARK-SUBMIT.mjs. Native journal time and frozen content define the observation boundary. Candidate-owned npm start is the older runtime, not this protocol.

Keep the historical proofs separate

The saved profile, cold-recovery and regression examples remain demonstrations of specific helpers at their recorded revision. They are not a new whole-harness evaluation or results from this kitchen test.

If a live view fails

Spend at most 30 seconds recovering. Return to the public requirement and evidence guide. Do not change a score, reset a clock, silently repair a frozen candidate or launch a paid model run to fill time.
