# Shared narrative structure

Use this method for a requested presentation or demonstration. Structure serves
the audience's actual decision or learning goal; a dramatic arc is not mandatory
for a tutorial, factual update or feature walkthrough.

## One structure pass

Choose the genre and reuse an already approved arc. Do not dispatch another
planner merely because the same structure will appear as slides and narration.
PPTNarrativeArchitect adds slide goals/layout/asset suggestions;
DemoNarrativeArc adds scene actions, transitions and available fallbacks.
Those are views of one arc, not two competing plans.

When a story serves the brief, consider these beats:

| Beat | Question |
|---|---|
| Opening | What supported stake or audience question makes this relevant? |
| Setup | What context is necessary before the proposed answer makes sense? |
| Escalation | Which evidenced constraint or alternative changes the decision? |
| Payoff | What actual demonstration, result or reasoning earns the answer? |
| Close | What authorized next action or takeaway follows? |

A missing beat is not permission to invent a customer failure, metric or
commitment. Omit or adapt it when the material cannot honestly support it.
A tutorial can use task -> demonstration -> check; an update can use
change -> evidence -> consequence. Record why that structure fits.

## Time and evidence

Allocate time from actual duration, delivery mode, reading/speaking needs,
demonstrations, transitions and questions. Percentage splits, slide counts and
an opening-time heuristic are not measured pacing or mandatory quotas.
Retain approved facts, citations, limitations and long-form supporting detail.
Source brevity must not become content loss.

Return an arc draft with content goals, supported claims, timing basis and
unresolved inputs. It is not final copy, a rendered deck or a rehearsal result.
The authorized caller persists it and assigns final writing/rendering.

For critique, use the supplied arc or script and cite scene/slide/time locations.
Report a concrete structural problem and its source-preserving remedy. The
planner may self-check but cannot claim independent critique of its own arc.
Per-paragraph voice remains with the configured voice method; a structure pass
is not a voice, licensing or publication clearance.
