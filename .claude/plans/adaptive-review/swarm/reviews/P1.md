## Specification review

SPEC PASS for T1-T4. It was established in attempt 1 and still holds for attempt 2. Citations refer
to `lib/review_context.py` as integrated.

- **T1: the consequence decision.**
  - The six axes and their values match the spec exactly.
  - Boolean axes accept only a real `True`/`False` or `"unknown"`, so `1`, `0` and `"true"` are
    refused (`_fact_value`).
  - `schema_version` must be a real integer.
  - The six deep floors match the spec.
  - Lean requires every fact to be known and low-consequence.
  - Unknown or absent facts give at least standard, with `needs-context` and `risk-unknown`. No
    context at all gives standard.
  - A lower explicit depth raises `DepthRefused`; a higher one is allowed and recorded.
  - Known facts need nonblank evidence, and `asserted_by` needs both an actor and a reference.
  - Every result carries its source evidence, the tags, `release_clearance: false` and an
    all-false authority record.
- **T2: the regression matrix** covers the floors, unknowns, refusals, the boolean/integer cases,
  malformed shapes and the strict `read_context` boundary.
- **T3: the pattern-provider adapter.**
  - The provider loads lazily and only from the resolved sibling `patterns.py`, under a private
    module name. It never loads through `sys.path` or an ambient `patterns` module.
  - The Python 3.10 gate matches the frozen patterns documentation.
  - When the provider is missing, broken or incompatible, the result is `unavailable` before any
    input is read.
  - `verify_lock` must return `ok` before the task map is read and `project_package` is called.
    `review_coverage` runs only when coverage is supplied.
  - Clauses, settings, tasks, exceptions and `asset_pins` pass through unchanged. No assets are read.
- **T4: the provider-boundary tests** are labelled as using a provider double. They cover absence,
  ambient decoys, failed verification, a 300-clause verbatim projection and the propagation of
  coverage and error statuses.

## Quality review

QUALITY PASS. The history of each correction is preserved below.

- **Attempt 1, first-pass P3 findings:**
  1. **Verify warnings were dropped when coverage was supplied.** Fixed: verify diagnostics now
     always come first, followed by an exact-repeat `_dedupe`. A test covers no coverage, coverage,
     and `review-unmet`.
  2. **The renderer trusts a caller-supplied assessment.** Handed to T7 as a coordinator
     obligation: the packet CLI must call `assess_depth` on the raw, bound context and never render
     an externally supplied assessment. This review does not verify that.
  3. **The real-sibling test would skip once the provider was installed.** Fixed: it now checks
     whichever state is actually present (absent, unsupported Python, or trusted sibling) with no
     skip.
- **Attempt 2, parse-boundary correction:** `read_context` now re-raises `ContextError` first, so
  the duplicate-key and NaN/Infinity hook messages survive. It handles `UnicodeDecodeError` and
  `JSONDecodeError` next. It translates `RecursionError` and the remaining `ValueError` (including
  Python 3.11's integer digit limit) into `ContextError`. Three tests cover exact hook messages,
  10000-deep nesting within the size limit, and the 5000-digit integer on both kinds of
  interpreter, without a skip.

No open findings.
