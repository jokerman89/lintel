proven path-length cause or a successful prerequisite. The coordinator checked
the primary [CSIDL documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/shell/csidl):

initializing that folder. It is a diagnostic, never a substitute for Chrome's
normal check.
