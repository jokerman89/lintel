## Compatibility and operator action

Public entrypoint names, work-map/evidence/profile schema versions, pack
inheritance and the retained role capabilities do not change. Unsupported
inputs produce explicit failures rather than selecting a fallback output or
implying verification. This preserves the existing forward-compatibility
boundary; it does not establish support for an unobserved client or provider.

No data or configuration migration is introduced. Existing managed consumers
update through their normal explicitly selected source/target adapter operation
and its separate owned recovery store, then run the existing integrity check.
Local edits remain conflicts; regeneration does not authorize replacing them.
Required profile references must still verify, and no automatic rebind or
transfer follows from a source update. The isolated bisect helper now requires
native drive/UNC normalization through the existing MSYS/Cygwin `cygpath` tool;
unavailable normalization stops before worktree creation.

Any rollback needs its own scoped authorization, an ordinary corrective/revert
commit and regenerated consumer output with fresh applicable verification.
Preserve original reports, profile generations and recovery snapshots. Do not
reset/rewrite history, silently reuse stale acceptance or automatically remove
trials and recovery data.
