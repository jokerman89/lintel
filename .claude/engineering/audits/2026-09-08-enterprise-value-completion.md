## Resulting enterprise behavior

selection and plan acceptance. PLAN maps each applicable requirement to existing task/package
IDs and evidence. Missing executable capabilities remain visible rather than being counted as
passed controls. Team overlays preserve inherited requirements unless a whole block is explicitly
overridden. The machine's active pointer and a session's cached identity have distinct lifetimes.

package, aggregate risk, independent specification then quality review, and evidence for every
leaf. A failed leaf keeps its package open; verification-only work has a valid evidence path.
Original Spec Kit task IDs and artifacts remain authoritative when that bridge is selected.
